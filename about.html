About PixelPress

Last updated: October 22, 2026

PixelPress is a simple image compression tool designed to help people reduce image file sizes directly in their web browser.

How it works

Choose supported images, select compression settings, and download the results. Image processing is performed locally in your browser by the tool; image files are not intentionally uploaded to a PixelPress server.

Our goal

We aim to provide a straightforward, accessible, and privacy-conscious image utility. Results depend on the image, selected format, and browser capabilities.
