QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Is PixelPress really free?

Yes. This version has no account, usage fee, or paid API. Hosting and domain costs may apply if you choose paid services.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image processing runs in your browser. Your browser downloads the compression library from a CDN when the page loads, but your image files are not sent to that CDN by this app.

Why did my PNG get larger?

PNG is a lossless format. Some images do not compress well, and converting formats can change transparency or increase file size. Try WebP for smaller files when appropriate.

Does it support every image format?

Support depends on your browser. The app accepts common image files, but animated GIFs, HEIC, SVG, and some AVIF files may not behave as expected. This tool is intended for raster images.

Will the result always be smaller?

No. Compression results depend on the image, output format, dimensions, and browser encoder. The app keeps the output even if it is larger, and shows the size difference.
