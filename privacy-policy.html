Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how PixelPress may handle information when you visit this website and use its image compressor.

Images you select

The compressor processes selected images in your browser. PixelPress does not intentionally upload image files to its own server. Image previews and temporary output files are managed by your browser and can be cleared by refreshing or closing the page.

Information and logs

The website host may process standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. The exact data depends on your hosting provider and configuration.

Cookies and advertising

PixelPress may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, if enabled. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads as allowed by your settings and applicable law. Third-party providers process data under their own policies.

Google explains its data use in Advertising and its Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Where required, the site will provide a consent mechanism before using non-essential cookies or personalized advertising. Disabling cookies may affect some third-party features.

Children

This tool is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the service or legal requirements change. For privacy questions, use the contact details published on our Contact page.
